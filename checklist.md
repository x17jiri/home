# Checklist: povolení záměru pro nový rodinný dům

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

This checklist is intended for an ordinary new detached house in the Czech Republic under Act No. 283/2021 Sb. The current formal procedure is **povolení stavby nebo zařízení** (commonly still called *stavební povolení*) within the unified *povolení záměru* process.

Items marked **if applicable** depend on the plot, utility connections and proposed equipment. Confirm the final set with the responsible designer and the locally competent building authority.

## 1. Initial checks

- [ ] Identify the competent building authority (*stavební úřad*).
- [ ] Confirm that the house and all associated structures are included in one project: house, utility connections, driveway, paved areas, retaining structures, rainwater system, well/WWTP, fences, heat-pump outdoor unit, etc.
- [ ] Check compliance with the current zoning plan, regulatory plan and local planning requirements.
- [ ] Check ownership, easements and other registered rights for every affected parcel.
- [ ] Check whether construction or utility routes require access to neighbouring land.
- [ ] Decide between the ordinary and accelerated proceeding (*zrychlené řízení*).
- [ ] **Optional:** obtain preliminary information from the building authority (*předběžná informace*).
- [ ] **If applicable:** identify any required exception from construction requirements (*výjimka z požadavků na výstavbu*).

## 2. Application and administrative attachments

- [ ] Completed official **Žádost o povolení stavby nebo zařízení** – Annex No. 3 to Decree No. 149/2024 Sb.
- [ ] Applicant identification and contact details.
- [ ] Identification of all affected parcels and existing affected structures.
- [ ] Basic description, extent and purpose of the project.
- [ ] Proposed construction method: contractor or self-build (*dodavatelsky / svépomocí*).
- [ ] Expected construction start and completion dates.
- [ ] List of known proceeding participants (*seznam známých účastníků řízení*).
- [ ] **If represented:** power of attorney (*plná moc*).
- [ ] **If the applicant is not the owner and lacks a sufficient right of superficies/easement:** owner's consent under § 187, marked on the situation drawing and containing the owner's identification and signature.
- [ ] **If an affected existing structure is not registered in the Cadastre:** affidavit proving ownership.
- [ ] **For accelerated proceedings only:** consents of all proceeding participants, marked on the situation drawing with identification and signatures.
- [ ] **If applicable:** planning agreement (*plánovací smlouva*), particularly where public infrastructure must be built or modified.
- [ ] **If applicable:** description and justification of a requested exception from construction requirements.
- [ ] **If applicable:** identification and justification for temporary use of neighbouring land or a neighbouring structure during construction.
- [ ] Pay the administrative fee when requested by the authority.

## 3. Documentation for the permit

Documentation should follow Decree No. 131/2024 Sb., normally be prepared by authorised designers, bear the required electronic authorisations and be uploaded to the Evidence of Electronic Documentation (*Evidence elektronických dokumentací, EED*).

### A. Průvodní list

- [ ] Identification of the project, site and affected parcels.
- [ ] Identification and authorisation numbers of the lead designer and specialist designers.
- [ ] Identification of the authorised surveyor, where relevant.
- [ ] List of input documents.
- [ ] Technical-economic building attributes (*TEA*).
- [ ] Attributes needed for utility connection and protection-zone conditions.

### B. Souhrnná technická zpráva

- [ ] Description of the site, proposed house and compliance with planning documentation.
- [ ] Conclusions of the relevant surveys.
- [ ] Overall architectural, building and technical solution.
- [ ] Accessibility and safety requirements.
- [ ] Structural concept and basic mechanical-resistance description.
- [ ] Fire-safety principles.
- [ ] Building physics: thermal protection, moisture, daylight/lighting, acoustics and vibration as relevant.
- [ ] Protection against radon, groundwater, flooding and other site effects.
- [ ] Utility connections and required capacities.
- [ ] Transport access, driveway and parking.
- [ ] Terrain, landscaping and vegetation solution.
- [ ] Environmental impacts and mitigation measures.
- [ ] Water management: potable water, wastewater and rainwater retention/use/infiltration/discharge.
- [ ] Construction organisation principles, site access, earthworks, temporary occupation and proposed inspection phases.

### C. Situační výkresy

- [ ] C.1 Situation of wider relations (*situační výkres širších vztahů*).
- [ ] C.2 Cadastral situation drawing (*katastrální situační výkres*).
- [ ] C.3 Coordination situation drawing, normally at no more than 1:200, showing levels, distances, all structures, connections, driveway, paved areas, terrain, fire access and site boundary.
- [ ] **If applicable:** C.4 special situation drawings for protection zones, environmental constraints, easements, geotechnical probes or construction organisation.
- [ ] **If applicable:** C.5 parcel division or consolidation drawing.

### D. Documentation of objects

- [ ] Architectural/building technical report.
- [ ] Plans of every storey with room uses and the structural concept.
- [ ] Characteristic sections, including foundation and terrain relationships.
- [ ] All principal elevations and integration into the surroundings.
- [ ] Roof plan and other drawings needed to understand the proposal.
- [ ] Basic description/drawings of technological and building-service equipment.
- [ ] Structural technical report (*stavebně konstrukční řešení*).
- [ ] Basic structural calculation (*základní statický výpočet*), including foundations and the principal load-bearing elements.
- [ ] Foundation and load-bearing-structure drawings.
- [ ] Fire safety report (*požárně bezpečnostní řešení, PBŘ*), where required in the prescribed scope.

### Energy and building systems

- [ ] Energy performance certificate (*PENB*) and demonstration of compliance with energy requirements.
- [ ] Thermal assessment and construction build-ups.
- [ ] Water supply and internal plumbing concept.
- [ ] Foul-water drainage concept.
- [ ] Rainwater retention, use, infiltration or controlled-discharge design and calculations.
- [ ] Heating and domestic-hot-water concept, including the proposed energy source.
- [ ] Ventilation/heat-recovery concept.
- [ ] Electrical supply and installation concept.
- [ ] Lightning protection concept, if required by the risk assessment.
- [ ] **If applicable:** gas installation.
- [ ] **If applicable:** photovoltaic system or other local energy source.
- [ ] **If applicable:** chimney and flue solution.

### Submission form

- [ ] Every documentation part is coordinated and uses the same building position, levels and dimensions.
- [ ] Every responsible designer has applied the required authorisation/signature.
- [ ] Electronic documentation has been uploaded to EED through the Building Administration Portal.
- [ ] The EED documentation ID is entered in the application.

## 4. Surveys and input documents

- [ ] Current cadastral map and ownership/easement review.
- [ ] Surveyed site plan with planimetric and elevation data (*polohopis a výškopis*).
- [ ] Available utility data, including the Digital Technical Map (*DTM*).
- [ ] Geotechnical/engineering-geological survey sufficient to design the foundations.
- [ ] Radon index survey of the building site.
- [ ] **If infiltration is proposed:** hydrogeological assessment and/or infiltration test.
- [ ] **If a well is proposed:** hydrogeological assessment and well documentation.
- [ ] **If an on-site WWTP, septic tank or holding tank is proposed:** relevant hydrogeological and wastewater documentation.
- [ ] **If significant trees are affected:** tree survey/arboricultural assessment.
- [ ] **If an existing structure is changed or demolished:** measured survey and structural/building-condition survey.
- [ ] **If required by the site:** flood, mining, slope-stability, contamination, archaeological or other specialist survey.

## 5. Public transport and technical infrastructure

Obtain statements from every owner/operator identified as affected in the DTM, including both connection conditions and protection-zone conditions.

- [ ] Electricity distribution operator statement and connection conditions.
- [ ] Water-supply operator/owner statement and connection conditions.
- [ ] Sewer operator/owner statement and connection conditions.
- [ ] Electronic communications network statements, including CETIN and any other affected operator.
- [ ] **If applicable:** gas distribution operator statement.
- [ ] **If applicable:** district heating/cooling operator statement.
- [ ] **If applicable:** municipal networks, public lighting, drainage, irrigation, pipelines or other infrastructure statements.
- [ ] Confirmation that proposed work in every affected protection or safety zone is acceptable.
- [ ] **For a new or modified driveway:** road owner consent and the required road authority/police statement or access permit.
- [ ] Evidence of lawful permanent access from the house to a public road.
- [ ] **If connections cross third-party land:** owner consent and the required easement/agreement.

## 6. Authorities and environmental documents

Determine which items apply to this particular parcel. A normal family house will not require every item below.

- [ ] Determine whether a **Jednotné environmentální stanovisko (JES)** is required.
- [ ] **If applicable:** JES, including consent to remove land from the Agricultural Land Fund (*ZPF*).
- [ ] **If applicable:** water-authority statement/decision or water-use permit for a well, discharge, WWTP or other water-related project.
- [ ] **If applicable:** forestry authority document when the project affects forest land or lies within the relevant distance from a forest.
- [ ] **If applicable:** nature-protection documents for tree felling, landscape character, significant landscape features, protected species, protected areas or Natura 2000.
- [ ] **If applicable:** heritage authority binding opinion for a cultural monument, conservation area or conservation zone.
- [ ] **If applicable:** Fire Rescue Service (*HZS*) opinion where state fire supervision applies.
- [ ] **If applicable:** public-health authority (*KHS*) opinion, for example where noise or another protected public-health interest is affected.
- [ ] **If applicable:** transport/road authority documents beyond the utility/driveway statements above.
- [ ] **If applicable:** opinions relating to aviation, railways, mining areas, spa or water-source protection zones, flood areas, defence infrastructure or other special constraints.
- [ ] **If applicable:** EIA screening conclusion or EIA opinion. This is normally not relevant to an ordinary detached house.
- [ ] **If applicable:** other decision or binding opinion identified by the designer, JES authority or building authority.

Under § 184(3) of the Building Act, the building authority can request a missing required statement or binding opinion itself. Supplying a complete set with the application will usually make the process easier; standalone decisions and applicant-specific supporting material may still have to be obtained by the applicant.

## 7. Final pre-submission review

- [ ] Parcel numbers, cadastral area, applicant names and project title match in every document.
- [ ] Building footprint, distances, finished-floor level, ridge height and terrain levels agree across all drawings and reports.
- [ ] Utility routes and capacities agree across the situation drawing, reports and operator statements.
- [ ] Rainwater calculations agree with the site plan and hydrogeological conclusions.
- [ ] Structural dimensions and loads agree with the architectural documentation.
- [ ] PENB inputs agree with the actual wall, floor, roof, window and system specifications.
- [ ] PBŘ assumptions agree with layouts, structures, fire separations, access and water supply.
- [ ] All opinions and statements are still valid and all their conditions are incorporated into the project.
- [ ] The list of participants is complete.
- [ ] For accelerated proceedings, all required participant consents are present and correctly signed on the situation drawing.
- [ ] The complete application and attachments are submitted via the Building Administration Portal, data box, post or in person.

## 8. Needed later, but normally not as permit-application attachments

- [ ] Detailed documentation for construction of the family house under Annex No. 9 to Decree No. 131/2024 Sb., completed before construction starts.
- [ ] Final contractor details, or the authorised site manager/supervisor required for self-build.
- [ ] Notification of the construction start and other information required by the permit.
- [ ] Setting-out documentation and authorised surveyor's setting-out record.
- [ ] Site sign and construction diary.
- [ ] Inspections, tests, certificates and revision reports generated during construction.
- [ ] As-built survey and Digital Technical Map update.
- [ ] Documentation and application required for occupancy approval (*kolaudační rozhodnutí*).
